# Junior Trader – Example Markets (fictional demo posting)

Location: London, UK

## About the role
Example Markets is hiring a Junior Trader to join its equity derivatives desk. You will support senior
traders with trade execution, daily risk monitoring and market analysis across equities, futures and
options.

## Responsibilities
- Execute trades in equities, futures and options under the supervision of senior traders.
- Monitor daily P&L, margin and position limits, and escalate limit breaches.
- Prepare end-of-day risk summaries and trade reviews for the desk.
- Produce short market notes covering macro data, earnings and price action.
- Keep trade records accurate in Excel and internal systems.

## Requirements
- A degree in finance, economics or a related subject.
- Some trading or market research experience, including internships.
- Strong Excel skills; basic Python is a plus.
- Understanding of options and futures.
- Clear written communication and attention to detail.
