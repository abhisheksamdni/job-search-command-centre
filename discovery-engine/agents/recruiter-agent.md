# recruiter-agent

Orchestrates [[senior-recruiter]] over the clean JD plus Active CV evidence. It owns the complete persistent Recruiter Intelligence Record, including the eight-class requirement taxonomy, evidence and source-quality grading, hard gates, interview defensibility, controlled improvement instructions and final recruiter judgement. Recruiter Match is primary; [[ats-exact]] consumes its terminology/evidence decisions as a separate discoverability check and does not redefine them.

Skills are the source of truth; this file only defines orchestration.
